How to use MFO-pro

Quick start
  1. Open Destinations and check each place: commission %, cargo fee per kg and currency.
  2. On New transaction, pick the destination and direction, then enter the date, transporter and customer.
  3. Enter the capital you used, the amount the customer paid and the total weight. Pick a currency for each amount if it wasn’t RM.
  4. Check the exchange rate. It fills in from the live rates. Type your own to override it (the label changes to “manual”). Press Use live rate to go back.
  5. Read the calculation beside the form (below it on a phone).
  6. Press Generate invoices. Switch between Customer, Operator statement and Transporter, then press Print / save as PDF and Save to history.

Press Load test example to fill in a sample transaction: capital RM153, customer paid RM305.64, 10.2 kg at 2.83.

What each input means
  • Capital: your own money used to buy the items. Choose the currency you spent. The box beside it shows the amount in RM as you type.
  • Customer paid: what the customer paid you for the items and delivery. Choose the currency they paid in, for example AUD. The box beside it shows the RM amount, rounded to the nearest sen. The destination’s currency uses the Rate field (including your manual change); other currencies use the live rates.
  • Total weight: kilos carried by the transporter.
  • Rate: RM for 1 unit of the destination’s currency, used to turn the per-kg fee into RM.
  • Payment method: bank transfer or cash, shown on the invoices.
  • Transporter payout: set to Paid once you have paid the transporter. The payout statement shows PAID or PENDING.
How the amounts are worked out
Profit = customer paid − capital
Commission = commission % × profit (or × amount paid, if the destination is set that way)
Incentive = weight × fee per kg, converted to RM at the rate
Commission + incentive = transporter total, rounded to the nearest RM for the payout
You keep = customer paid − capital − payout

Example, ADL: profit 305.64 − 153 = 152.64. Commission 58% = 88.53. Incentive 10.2 kg × AUD 5 = AUD 51 × 2.83 = 144.33. Total 232.86, paid out as RM233.

If the payout is higher than the profit, a warning appears so you can check the numbers before you invoice.

The three invoices
  • Customer: items, weight, route and total paid. It does not show your capital or profit.
  • Operator statement: your full breakdown, including profit, commission, incentive and what you keep.
  • Transporter: the commission and incentive working, the rounded payout and whether it is paid.

Each invoice gets a number like MFO-ADL-261113-001. Use your browser’s print dialog to save as PDF.

Destinations and rules
  • Press Add destination and fill in the code, city, currency, commission % and fee per kg.
  • Applies to sets whether a rule covers both directions or only one. A one-direction rule overrides the “both” rule for that direction.
  • Transporter pay: commission % plus a cargo fee per kg, which is what you pay the transporter. The fee can be in any currency, and it can change above a set weight, applied to the whole load or only the extra kg.
  • Doha example: cargo fee QAR 25 per kg for 10 kg or less, and QAR 20 per kg on the whole load above 10 kg. So 10 kg = QAR 250 and 11 kg = QAR 220. The transporter gets 58% commission plus this cargo fee.
  • Whatever is left after paying the transporter is yours.
  • Use Copy to start a new destination from a similar one.
  • Changes apply to new transactions. Saved invoices keep the rules they were made with.
Exchange rates

The Exchange rates tab shows the latest rates against MYR with their date. They update when you open the app online and are kept for offline use. Press Refresh rates to update by hand.

History

Saved transactions appear in History. Open one to see its invoices again or to reuse its details. Delete removes it from this device.

Backups: keeping your settings safe
  • Everything is stored in this browser on this device only.
  • A backup file downloads each time you save a destination. Keep the latest one in a safe place, such as your cloud drive.
  • On a new device, open Destinations, press Import backup and choose the file.
  • The Last backups list lets you restore or download recent versions.
Day and night mode

Press the button at the top right. It follows your phone’s setting until you choose, then remembers your choice. Printed invoices always stay white.
